Ḥida, Ma'agal Tov

The travel diary of Ḥayyim Yosef David Azulai, with an English translation paragraph by paragraph · מעגל טוב

Ḥayyim Yosef David Azulai (the Ḥida, 1724–1806) travelled twice as an emissary of the Jews of Hebron and the Land of Israel, in the 1750s and the 1770s, through Egypt, Italy, Germany, Holland, France, England and Tunisia. His diary, Ma'agal Tov ("the good circuit"), records the money he raised, the scholars he argued with, the libraries and courts he saw, and his own grief and melancholy. It was not printed in his lifetime.

The Hebrew text

  • Base text: the printing of Przemyśl, 1885 (Sefer Ma'agal Tov), which holds part of the second journey, from Tunis to Italy. Scan: archive.org, maagaltob00azul (University of Illinois, 650 ppi). The scan lacks the title page and starts in the middle of a passage.
  • OCR: Kraken with the PP-OCRv6 model, line by line, then checked against the scan by eye. Spelling, abbreviations and punctuation are as printed.
  • Complete text: A. Freimann's edition, Ma'agal Tov ha-Shalem (Mekize Nirdamim, Berlin 1921 and Jerusalem 1934), holds both journeys. Its only scan found online (HebrewBooks) is too poor for OCR at present.

Method

  • Rows. One row is one paragraph of the print. The print ends each entry with a colon.
  • English. Plain and close to the Hebrew. Dates by the weekly Torah portion are kept ("Tuesday of the week of Shemini").
  • Uncertain renderings have the mark ⚠ and a note at the end of the page; unresolved abbreviations are left as uncertain.
  • Translator. The translation is by Claude (Anthropic), directed and reviewed by Ezra Brand.
