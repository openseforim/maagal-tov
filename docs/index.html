Ḥida, Ma'agal Tov

The travel diary of Ḥayyim Yosef David Azulai, with an English translation paragraph by paragraph · מעגל טוב

Ḥayyim Yosef David Azulai (the Ḥida, 1724–1806) travelled twice as an emissary of the Jews of Hebron and the Land of Israel, in the 1750s and the 1770s, through Egypt, Italy, Germany, Holland, France, England and Tunisia. His diary, Ma'agal Tov ("the good circuit"), records the money he raised, the scholars he argued with, the libraries and courts he saw, and his own grief and melancholy. It was not printed in his lifetime.

The title page, the two prefaces and the opening of the diary are in the front matter.

Background for the reader: the Ḥida's missions, Hebron and the emissaries, the places he passed through, dates and hours, money, people and titles, travel, books, and the history of the text, with a list of further reading.

Index of people and places: the Ḥida's family, the rabbis, gevirim and noblemen he met, the earlier authors he names, and the places, each with links to the paragraphs.

The Hebrew text

  • Base text: the printing of Przemyśl, 1885 (Sefer Ma'agal Tov), which reprints an earlier Italian edition (its editor says in his preface that he brought the autograph to Livorno in Tammuz 5627, that is 1867). It holds the second journey, 1773–1778 (Egypt, Tunis, Italy, France, Holland, Paris again, Turin, and the return to Livorno), a few later entries to 1793, and the editors' additions: family memories of the Ḥida (Shem Tov), his private notes (Sefer ha-Zikkaron) and stories about him. The first journey is not in it. Scan: archive.org, maagaltob00azul (University of Illinois, 650 ppi).
  • Order of the leaves. The leaves of the scanned copy are bound out of order. Scans 34–39 (viewer numbers) are the title page, the prefaces and the opening of the diary; some leaves after them are lost, and the text takes up again in Tunis at scan 7. Scan 33 goes on directly to scan 40. The reader follows the order of the text, and each part lists its scans.
  • OCR: Kraken with the PP-OCRv6 model, line by line, then checked against the scan by eye. Spelling, abbreviations and punctuation are as printed.
  • Complete text: A. Freimann's edition, Ma'agal Tov ha-Shalem (Mekize Nirdamim, Berlin 1921 and Jerusalem 1934), holds both journeys. Its only scan found online (HebrewBooks) is too poor for OCR at present.

Method

  • Rows. One row is one paragraph of the print. The print ends each entry with a colon.
  • English. Plain and close to the Hebrew. Dates by the weekly Torah portion are kept ("Tuesday of the week of Shemini").
  • Uncertain renderings have the mark ⚠ and a note at the end of the page; unresolved abbreviations are left as uncertain.
  • Translator. The translation is by Claude (Anthropic), directed and reviewed by Ezra Brand.
