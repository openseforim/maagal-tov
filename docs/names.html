Index of people, places and books

The people, places and books named in the English translation, with links to the paragraphs

About this index

307 entries, made by matching name forms in the English. A short form such as "R. Moses" or "Signor David" is counted only where it means the person of the entry. Mentions by title only ("the gevir", "the rabbi", "my son") and bare initials ("R. Y. B.") are not indexed, and the notes on uncertain renderings are not searched. The Ḥida himself writes "I"; his entry lists only the editors' parts. Identifications in the entries come from the text, with a few well-known ones added (for example, R. Saul of Amsterdam is Saul Löwenstamm). Where a name is abbreviated or torn, the entry may follow an educated guess that the note on that passage explains; such guesses are marked "probably". Books of the Bible and the Talmud are not listed. Numbers are paragraph numbers; front matter in roman numerals.
