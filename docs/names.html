About this index

1604 entries, made by matching name forms in the English. A short form such as "R. Moses" or "Signor David" is counted only where it means the person of the entry. Mentions by title only ("the gevir", "the rabbi", "my son") and bare initials ("R. Y. B.") are not indexed, and the notes on uncertain renderings are not searched. The Ḥida himself writes "I"; his entry lists only the editors' parts. Identifications in the entries come from the text, with a few well-known ones added (for example, R. Saul of Amsterdam is Saul Löwenstamm). Where a name is abbreviated or torn, the entry may follow an educated guess that the note on that passage explains; such guesses are marked "probably". Books of the Bible and the Talmud are not listed. Numbers are paragraph numbers: in the 1885 printing as in its parts (front matter in roman numerals); in the full diary from Freimann's edition, page and paragraph ("103.2" = the second paragraph that begins on Freimann's p. 103). The same person can appear in both, since many entries are in both texts.
