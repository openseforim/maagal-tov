This is the Sefer ha-Zikkaron [Book of Remembrance] which the Lord brought to my hands [Exod 21:13] from the holy autograph itself of the Rav, the Ḥida, may the memory of the righteous and holy be a blessing: the order of his conduct and his holiness during his mission. He used to write it on slips of paper, to be a reminder to him and to add to his holiness. May his merit protect us, amen.

In the year 5534 [1773–74] I completed the Ḥok le-Yisrael, most of it on the night of Hoshana Rabba. I limited my speech from Rosh Hashanah until the end of Yom Kippur. I took care not to drink even water outside the sukkah, and not to sleep outside the sukkah. I fasted from the eve of Rosh Hashanah until Yom Kippur. I fasted on some Fridays in the weeks from the portion Vayera to Va'era [November 1773 to January 1774]. I read Rashi on Ecclesiastes and the Song of Songs, some of the Shoḥer Tov, and the Kitzur Semag. I read the Or ha-Ḥayyim from the portion Aḥarei to the portion Shelaḥ. On 5 Shevat [Monday 17 January 1774] I completed the twenty-four [books of the Bible]. On the night of Shavuot, in the lazaretto [the evening of Sunday 15 May 1774], I read the Magen Avot of Rashbatz ⚠1 and the whole of Ekhah Rabbati; and most of the Ḥok le-Yisrael up to Re'eh. From Ḥeshvan I took care, as far as I could, about eating meat on weekdays, and for a long time also about a dish cooked with meat.

In the year "an inheritance of the congregation" [Deut 33:4; קהלת = 535: 5535, 1774–75], with God's help. On both nights of Rosh Hashanah I read the whole book of Psalms each night before sleeping, and by day, each day, another time. On the night before Friday, 4 Tishrei [the evening of Thursday 8 September 1774], I prayed ⚠2, and also on the night before Sunday, 6 Tishrei [the evening of Saturday 10 September 1774]. With God's help I fasted from the eve of Rosh Hashanah until Yom Kippur, and all the food that I ate from Rosh Hashanah until the eve of Yom Kippur I ate with the intentions for eating. I much limited my speech from Rosh Hashanah until the end of Yom Kippur. I read the chapters on repentance from the Ḥemdat Yamim ⚠3 and some of the chapters on Yom Kippur. On Yom Kippur I read Psalms and the two Idrot. I read the Mekhilta as rapid reading. On the night of Hoshana Rabba, with God's help, I did the Tikkun. I did not drink even water outside the sukkah. I completed the Sha'arei Kedushah. I read the Birkat Avraham of R. A. B. ⚠4. I read the Orḥot Tzaddikim, part 2, and the Ḥemdat Yamim from Ḥanukkah on. From the night of Tuesday of Vayeḥi until the night of the Sabbath [the evening of Monday 12 December to the evening of Friday 16 December 1774] I did not drink water. From Tuesday of Vayeḥi [13 December 1774] I went back to reading the Ḥok le-Yisrael. I read the commentary on the Haggadah by Rashbatz, may his soul rest in Eden.

In the year "And remember all the commandments of the Lord" [Num 15:39; מצות = 536: 5536, 1775–76], with God's help. On Rosh Hashanah I studied the Psalms by night and by day, twice each, which makes four times. I fasted from the eve of Rosh Hashanah until Yom Kippur. I read the Minhagei Maharil from [the section on] repentance to Ḥanukkah. I read the chapters on the Days of Repentance from the Ḥemdat Yamim, and three chapters on Yom Kippur. I much limited my speech from Rosh Hashanah until the end of Yom Kippur. I read about a third of the printed Sefer ha-Kavvanot. In the Days of Repentance I gave charity, and oil for the lamp, for the rest of the souls of my father and teacher, and my mother and teacher, etc. On the night of Yom Kippur I read Psalms and the Ne'dar ba-Kodesh ⚠5, and on the day of Yom Kippur too I read Psalms and the Ne'dar ba-Kodesh. I read the aggadot of the Yerushalmi in the book Yefeh Mar'eh. On Sukkot I read the Ḥemed Elohim. With God's help I did not drink even water, and did not sleep, outside the sukkah. On the night of Hoshana Rabba, with God's help, I read twenty-three folios of the Tikkunei Zohar. I completed the Targum and the D. H. ⚠6 and the Midrash Rabbah from the portion Vayetze to the end of Va'era. I read the Ein Yisrael and the Mishnat Ḥasidim. On Purim I read a commentary on the Scroll of Esther. On 16 Shevat [Tuesday 6 February 1776] I was made sandak at Ancona for the son of Signor Samuel Alamia ⚠7, and they named him Ḥayyim Yosef David. I read most of the Ḥok le-Yisrael, part 1. I read Rashi's Sefer ha-Orah in manuscript. I fasted on the eve of Passover [Wednesday 3 April 1776]. I told of the Exodus from Egypt ⚠8 until morning. On the night of Shavuot [the evening of Thursday 23 May 1776] I did as is my custom, and in the morning I immersed seven times, although I was suffering in my bowels and my teeth; and I did not speak at all during the night. I was called up to the Torah for the Ten Commandments and read them in the Spanish holy congregation ⚠9 in the city of Ferrara. From the eve of Passover to the eve of Shavuot I read the Midrash Ruth Rabbah, and with God's help I went through most of the book Ḥelek Benei Yehudah. On the day after the festival of Shavuot [Sunday 26 May 1776], in Ferrara, I was sandak for the son of Solomon Moses Cavaliero, and he was named Ḥ. Y. D. [Ḥayyim Yosef David]. In Av [July–August 1776] I was in Mantua; I read the book of Rav Amram in manuscript. I went through the book Derekh Yashar by R. Isaiah of ⚠10, of blessed memory, which was printed in the year [5]336 [1575–76].

In the year 5537 [1776–77], with God's help. In the Days of Repentance I studied the Psalms six times and sixty-one psalms more. I fasted from the eve of Rosh Hashanah until Yom Kippur. I much limited my speech from Rosh Hashanah until the end of Yom Kippur. In the Days of Repentance I gave charity, and oil for the lamp, for the rest of the souls of my father and teacher, my mother and teacher, and K. B. and M. V. ⚠11, etc. On the night and the day of Yom Kippur I read the two Idrot. On Sukkot I read some of the Ḥemed Elohim. I read the Midrash be-Ḥiddush of the Rav, of blessed memory ⚠12. I did not drink even water outside the sukkah, except for one day's meal in the house because of the rain; and likewise I slept a little [indoors] because of the rain, as those who suffer discomfort may. On the night of Hoshana Rabba and the day of Hoshana Rabba, with God's help, I limited my speech. I read the Shikheḥat Leket. On 12 Tevet [Sunday 22 December 1776], with God's help, I completed the Midrash Shoḥer Tov. I was also sandak for the son of Solomon Franco de Almeida. On the night of Shavuot [the evening of Tuesday 10 June 1777] I did not doze ⚠13 at all and did not speak at all. I read the order [of the night] and the twenty-four [books], the Idra Zuta, sayings [of the sages] and some novellae, and I immersed in the morning. I was called up for the Ten Commandments. During the day I did not speak at all. I read the Kitzur Semag, the Avot de-Rabbi Natan, the tractates Kallah and Soferim, and the Idra Rabba and Zuta ⚠14; the weekly portion, the Yalkut on Ruth and the Azharot. On the second day [Thursday 12 June 1777] I read the Yalkut on Proverbs up to chapter 12. With God's help I read the Menorat ha-Ma'or to the end of the fourth Lamp. I read the Ḥok le-Yisrael to the end of Bamidbar; the proems of Ekhah Rabbati. On [the Sabbath] Naḥamu [16 August 1777] I read the Ten Commandments in Florence. And all the year, with God's help, I took care about eating meat on weekdays.

In the year "Upon the head of Joseph, and upon the crown of the head of him that was separate from his brothers" [Gen 49:26; Deut 33:16; = 5538, 1777–78 ⚠15] I took upon myself, without a vow: To guard greatly against pride and anger. To beware of vegetables and worms. To beware of evil speech and K. H. ⚠16, of lying and of making my own praises known, of flattery, mockery and gloom, and of gluttony. To be exact in the accounts of the mission, past and future, and in the accounts of the servant and of everyone who deals with him. To pay the debts in Egypt, etc. To give charity every day for my sister and my sons, may God keep them. Study, the remembrances ⚠17, for its own sake and in truth; care in prayer, in blessings and in tefillin. Rising early to the synagogue. To be among those who are insulted and do not insult [b. Shabbat 88b]. The Shema at bedtime, and to accept [on myself] the order of the four deaths [of the court], and to take great care in it, and to examine my deeds. To persevere in the study of moral instruction and in rapid reading. To keep impure thoughts far away. To judge [others] favorably [m. Avot 1:6]. To seek a way to immerse on the eve of the holy Sabbath. To shorten the mission as much as I can.

To write to my sons and daughters, may God keep them. To pray for them and for all that is theirs. To put the writings in order. To study the Zohar.
